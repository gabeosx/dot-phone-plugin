# Using Dots Phone

Dots Phone is an independent, self-hosted project. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI or Twilio. Product and company names identify
the services the project can use; they do not imply a partnership.

## Accounts and costs

You run Dots Phone with accounts you control and are responsible for charges
incurred on those accounts. These can include OpenAI model usage, Twilio phone
numbers, calls and streams, and hosting or tunnel services. Charges may accrue
even when a call does not complete its task.

Dots Phone's call-budget checks use estimates. They are not a guaranteed cap on
your provider bill. Set appropriate limits with your providers and review their
pricing and actual usage before enabling calls.

## Results and review

AI can mishear, misunderstand or return incomplete or incorrect information.
Call completion and task outcomes are not guaranteed. Review important results
and confirm consequential details before acting on them. Do not use Dots Phone
for emergencies or urgent safety-critical decisions.

## Permission to call and share information

Make only calls you are authorized to make. Limit disclosures and connected-source
access to information you are allowed to use for the task. Use only an approved identity and purpose; do not misrepresent who is calling.
Answer identity questions honestly and include any disclosures required for the
specific call. Obtain any consent required for calling, recording or processing
call audio. Follow applicable laws and the terms of the
providers you use. Requirements depend on the call's purpose and jurisdiction;
enabling a recording option does not establish permission to record.

Review the [privacy and retention guide](PRIVACY.md) before using personal or
sensitive information. The [quickstart](QUICKSTART.md) explains setup requirements
and starts with dialing disabled.
