## Timing for the proposed Friday launch

October 23, 2026 is during daylight saving time in Los Angeles and New York.
**00:01 in America/Los_Angeles converts to 03:01 EDT in America/New_York.**
This conversion is our calculation, not a Product Hunt guarantee. If the form
actually uses fixed UTC−08:00 PST instead, it converts to 04:01 EDT. Capture the
date/time shown by the scheduler and use that value for coverage. Do not silently
resolve Product Hunt's inconsistent timezone labels. The [runbook](RUNBOOK.md)
keeps the owner available across both possibilities.

This kit uses the stricter description length and five static gallery images so
the listing fits both descriptions of those fields. It does not require a video,
paid promotion, a paid hunter, or a vote campaign.
